# Selective DAgger for Annihilation Conversion

## Purpose

This panel is a controlled reinforcement-learning experiment, not a general
HexWars training recipe. Its causal question is:

> Can high-confidence search supervision on states induced by the learned
> policy's own late-game failures make the existing tactical-v2 actor convert
> favorable positions into annihilation wins?

The intervention changes the actor's supervised training distribution. It
holds fixed the tactical-v2 observation, the 1,288-action encoding, rewards,
Random opponent, standard board, HexCNN actor architecture, and deterministic
deployment policy. This makes the result interpretable:

- if held-out teacher accuracy and closed-loop wins both improve, learner-state
  distribution shift was likely an important cause of the conversion failure;
- if teacher accuracy improves but games do not, the flat action
  representation or actor capacity becomes the stronger hypothesis;
- if teacher accuracy remains poor, oracle quality, label ambiguity, or model
  capacity must be investigated before changing the action contract.

`panel.json` is the authoritative experiment definition.
`seed-banks.json` is the authoritative seed allocation. Both files are strict,
hash-bound inputs; unknown fields and causal drift fail validation.

## Background: behavioral cloning, covariate shift, and DAgger

Behavioral cloning (BC) fits a policy to state-action pairs generated by a
teacher. For observation \(o\), legal-action mask \(m\), and teacher action
\(a^*\), ordinary BC minimizes a supervised loss under the teacher's state
distribution:

```text
theta_BC = arg min_theta E_(o,m,a*)~d_teacher[-log pi_theta(a* | o,m)]
```

The learner is deployed under its own distribution, `d_pi`, not
`d_teacher`. A small error can move the learner into a state absent from the
demonstrations, where another error becomes more likely. This compounding
distribution mismatch is covariate shift.

Dataset Aggregation (DAgger) addresses the mismatch by rolling out the current
learner, asking a teacher what it would do in learner-visited states, and
aggregating those labels:

```text
D_k = D_0 union O_1 union ... union O_k
pi_(k+1) = SupervisedTrain(D_k, warm_start=pi_k)
```

`D_0` is the immutable original demonstration corpus and `O_k` is iteration
`k`'s learner-induced overlay. Crucially, the learner action advances the
environment. The teacher supplies a counterfactual label; it does not take
control of the game.

Full DAgger would label every learner state. That is unsafe here because the
current bounded-search teacher is demonstrated to be a strong conversion
teacher but a weak complete standard-game policy. Selective DAgger therefore
queries search only in a prespecified region where it is plausibly competent.
Eligibility changes the training curriculum, but eligibility bits are never
policy inputs and add no inference-time rule.

## Selective label eligibility

Let the health-adjusted material of seat \(j\) in pre-action state \(s\) be

```text
M_j(s) = sum_(u alive for j) cost(u) * hp(u) / max_hp(u)
         + points_weight * banked_points_j.
```

Let \(M^0_L + M^0_O\) be the initial material of both seats. The normalized
learner advantage is

```text
A(s) = [M_L(s) - M_O(s)] / max(1, M^0_L + M^0_O).
```

This is a selection diagnostic, not a reward term. For learner proposal
\(a_L\), canonical cycle key \(c(s)\), opponent living-unit count \(n_O(s)\),
and productive legal-action count \(q(s)\), eligibility is

```text
E(s,a_L) =
    [n_O(s) <= 1]
 OR [A(s) > 0]
 OR [episode_count(c(s)) == 2]
 OR [a_L == EndTurn AND q(s) > 0].
```

The four reasons are named conversion, favorable state, cycle warning, and
action waste. The cycle warning deliberately fires on the second occurrence;
the authoritative draw classifier declares cycling on the third. All reasons
that apply are retained. An exact canonical state is retained at most once per
episode, and all retained labels are trained even when learner and teacher
agree. Disagreement remains a diagnostic, not an inclusion rule.

For each iteration:

- deterministic learner versus Random, from reciprocal seats;
- 70% `standard-3v3` and 30% across the six declared near/far conversion
  profiles;
- at least 20,000 valid training labels, with a 2,000-game hard ceiling;
- exactly 2,000 held-out targeted labels, with a 200-game hard ceiling;
- stopping only after the current reciprocal map pair is complete.

Failure to reach a target before its ceiling fails the stage. The target is not
relaxed after observing results.

## Oracle preflight

One global teacher is selected before any DAgger collection. The two candidates
share depth 4 and the `material-plus-pursuit-v1` heuristic:

| Candidate | Expansion budget |
|---|---:|
| baseline | 512 |
| wider | 2,048 |

Each candidate plays the same 20 maps for each of these profiles, from both
seats:

1. `conversion-3v1-near`
2. `conversion-3v1-far`
3. `conversion-2v1-near`
4. `conversion-2v1-far`
5. `conversion-1v1-near`
6. `conversion-1v1-far`

This is 120 maps and 240 games per candidate. Every sampled state is queried
twice with the identical state and oracle configuration. Each returned command
must be legal under the authoritative mask, encode to the recorded action, and
round-trip through the tactical-v2 codec. The measured expansion count may not
exceed the candidate budget.

For candidate \(c\), define

```text
win_rate_c = wins_c / 240
throughput_c = valid_labels_c / in_process_benchmark_seconds_c.
```

Candidate eligibility is the conjunction

```text
win_rate_c >= 0.85
AND determinism_failures_c == 0
AND round_trip_failures_c == 0
AND valid_labels_c > 0
AND throughput_c >= 10 labels/second.
```

The inequalities are inclusive. Among eligible candidates, the lexicographic
selection order is:

1. higher pooled win rate;
2. fewer cycling draws;
3. higher labeling throughput;
4. smaller expansion budget.

No per-state teacher choice or post-hoc budget tuning is allowed. If neither
candidate is eligible, the experiment stops before collection or training.

Preflight evidence contains per-game traces and replays plus W/L/D,
seat-specific counts, paired-map counts, Wilson 95% intervals, cycling and
action-waste diagnostics, wasted EndTurns, query determinism, codec failures,
expansions, elapsed time, benchmark time, throughput, and the selected oracle.
For a binomial count \(x\) in \(n\) games, the reported Wilson interval uses
\(z=1.96\):

```text
center = (p_hat + z^2/(2n)) / (1 + z^2/n)
radius = z/(1 + z^2/n)
         * sqrt[p_hat(1-p_hat)/n + z^2/(4n^2)]
interval = [center-radius, center+radius].
```

The bounded-search source identity is pinned to the physical
`engine/HexWars.Engine/BoundedSearchAgent.cs` bytes as well as the repository
execution identity.

## Overlay schema and aggregation

Each accepted DAgger row retains:

- the exact policy observation and legal mask;
- learner action and authoritative learner command;
- teacher action and authoritative teacher command;
- all eligibility reasons, disagreement, and canonical state hash;
- normalized advantage, opponent unit count, and productive-action count;
- seed, seat, profile, round, decision index, and game identity;
- learner checkpoint/source identities;
- oracle type, depth, budget, heuristic, code identity, and actual expansions;
- scenario, contract, encoding, base-dataset, repository, and panel identities.

Illegal, non-round-tripping, or nondeterministic teacher output fails the game;
there is no EndTurn fallback. Iteration \(k\) trains on the base corpus plus
overlays `1..k`. Every optimizer batch has fixed source exposure:

| Source | Exposure |
|---|---:|
| original Greedy standard demonstrations | 49% |
| original bounded-search conversion demonstrations | 21% |
| cumulative selective-DAgger overlays | 30% |

The 49:21 split preserves the original 70:30 ratio inside the 70% base share.
DAgger rows are sampled uniformly after the one-state-per-episode cap; this
first experiment does not weight examples by disagreement or teacher score.
Held-out overlays are cumulative for early stopping and diagnostics but never
enter optimizer minibatches.

## Actor-only masked distillation

For actor logits \(z_\theta(o)\), legal mask \(m\), and teacher action \(a^*\),
define illegal logits as negative infinity. The objective is

```text
L(theta) =
  -log [ exp(z_theta(o)[a*])
         / sum_(a : m[a]=true) exp(z_theta(o)[a]) ].
```

Only the actor and shared feature extractor receive this supervised gradient.
The value head is neither a target nor an input to deterministic deployment.
Iteration 1 warm-starts from the frozen PPO actor; iteration 2 starts from actor
1, and iteration 3 starts from actor 2.

Training is locked to batch size 256, learning rate `3e-4`, at most 50 epochs,
patience 5 on cumulative held-out targeted negative log likelihood, model and
sampler seed 227, CUDA optimization, and canonical CPU publication. Save/reload
logits, epoch metrics, phase timings, throughput, and hardware/software
provenance are evidence, not optional logging.

## Closed-loop evaluation and decision rule

The starting checkpoint and actors 1-3 play the identical deterministic
development schedule: seeds `20,000,000-20,000,099`, `standard-3v3`, Random
opponent, both learner seats, for 200 games per candidate. Draws and losses are
both non-wins. Search and research observers are disabled.

Reports include W/L/D and Wilson intervals, seat asymmetry, paired map-seat
changes and exact sign tests, cycling and action-waste incidence, wasted
EndTurns, final and peak normalized advantage, rounds/decisions to victory, and
held-out teacher agreement by eligibility reason.

Development selection is highest win rate, then lower cycling, lower action
waste, then earlier iteration. A one-seed treatment succeeds only if:

```text
(win-rate gain >= 0.20 OR absolute win rate >= 0.65)
AND relative cycling reduction >= 0.50.
```

A 20-point gain can authorize replication but is not itself the winning-model
milestone. The milestone requires every independent replicate to win at least
65% and the pooled replicates to win at least 70%.

## Restart, publication, and failure semantics

Every stage writes beneath a deterministic `.staging` directory. It may publish
by atomic rename only after reopening all outputs, checking strict schemas,
rehashing every owned physical file, and reconstructing summary metrics from
the physical evidence.

An exact completed artifact is immutable and reusable with zero games or
epochs. Reuse requires identical repository, panel bytes, seed-bank bytes,
scenario, contract, encoding, learner, dataset, selected teacher, and schedule.
Changed identity requires a new output root. A destination and staging root may
not coexist ambiguously, and incompatible completed evidence is never
overwritten.

Interrupted or failed work may retain `diagnostic.json`, traces, and replays for
inspection, but it has no completion manifest and cannot be consumed as
complete. Operators determine completion from the validated manifest, never
from a process disappearing or a file merely existing.

## Non-goals and limits of inference

- no reward-shaping changes;
- no PPO continuation or PPO hyperparameter sweep;
- no tactical-v3 candidate/afterstate action representation;
- no self-play or opponent curriculum;
- no final-bank evaluation in this experiment;
- no inference-time scripted rule, observer, or search call;
- no claim of general board-size, roster, mechanic, or unit-design competence;
- no claim that a conversion-specialist search teacher is a strong full-game
  policy.

Before interpreting a result, a student should be able to answer three
questions: Which state distribution generated each label? Which variables
changed between baseline and treatment? Which held-out schedule, if any, was
consulted while making that change? Those answers distinguish a causal
experiment from an adaptive training anecdote.
