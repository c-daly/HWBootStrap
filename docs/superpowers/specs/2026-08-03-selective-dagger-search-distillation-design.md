# Selective DAgger Search Distillation

## Status

Approved conversational design. This specification defines the first
learner-state correction experiment after the physical checkpoint audit. It
does not authorize use of the final seed bank, model promotion, tactical-v3,
or unbounded hyperparameter tuning.

## Problem and evidence

The strongest physically audited learned candidate is seed 227 PPO at 38,912
steps. On 200 standard reciprocal development games it won 66, lost 2, and
drew 132. Cycling occurred in all 132 draws, and 75 games contained material
action waste. Its drawn games nevertheless ended with mean normalized
advantage `+0.220`. The policy commonly wins exchanges without converting the
remaining advantage into annihilation.

The audit also changed how bounded search should be interpreted. Depth-4,
512-expansion bounded search previously won 93.3% of the declared conversion
suite, but it won only 53 of 200 full standard games in the audit. The search
implementation was explicitly built as a conversion teacher. In wide standard
states its fixed budget and deterministic command order can exhaust the budget
before exploring the complete root action set. It is therefore evidence for
local conversion competence, not a generally superior full-game policy.

Ordinary behavioral cloning learned from states generated by scripted teachers.
After the learned policy makes a different action, it enters states that those
demonstrations may not cover. This is sequential covariate shift: a small
classification error changes the future input distribution, which can compound
into behavior that supervised training never corrected.

DAgger addresses this by rolling out the learner, asking a teacher what it
would do in learner-visited states, and adding those labeled states to the
supervised dataset:

```text
D_(k+1) = D_k union {(observation, legal mask, teacher action)}
```

This experiment uses selective DAgger. It labels only states associated with
conversion, cycling, material advantage, or premature EndTurn. Selection
changes the training curriculum; it is never exposed to the deployed policy
as a feature or rule.

## Goal and causal question

The immediate question is:

> Can high-confidence search supervision on learner-induced failure states
> make the existing tactical-v2 actor convert favorable games into
> annihilation victories?

The intervention holds the observation, 1,288-way action encoding, reward,
opponent, standard board, and HexCNN actor architecture fixed. A positive
result identifies learner-state distribution shift as a major cause. High
held-out teacher accuracy without closed-loop improvement instead points toward
the flat action representation or model capacity.

## Non-goals

- No reward shaping changes.
- No ordinary PPO continuation or PPO hyperparameter sweep.
- No tactical-v3 candidate/afterstate implementation.
- No self-play or opponent curriculum.
- No use of locked final seeds `17,000,000-17,000,249`.
- No claim of general board-size, roster, mechanic, or unit-design competence.
- No inference-time scripted rules or search calls.

## Alternatives considered

### Full DAgger with the existing teacher

Labeling every learner decision is the most direct covariate-shift experiment,
but the standard-game audit shows that the present teacher is weaker than the
learner as a complete policy. Uniformly distilling it could erase useful opening
and combat behavior.

### Improve a general search oracle first

A stronger search implementation could support canonical all-state DAgger and
future tactical-v3 ranking labels. It is a larger independent research project
and delays the fastest test of the known late-game failure.

### Selected approach: selective DAgger

Use the immutable original demonstrations to preserve ordinary play, and use
search only to label learner-induced states within its demonstrated area of
competence. This is the smallest causal experiment that directly targets the
observed failure.

## Selective collection contract

The learner plays deterministically against Random from both reciprocal seats.
Start profiles use the existing 70% standard and 30% declared near/far
conversion distribution. The learner action, never the teacher action, advances
the game.

A learner decision is eligible for a teacher label when at least one condition
holds before the learner action is applied:

1. **Conversion:** the opponent has at most one living unit.
2. **Favorable state:** the learner has positive health-and-point-adjusted
   normalized advantage under the existing authoritative diagnostic.
3. **Cycle warning:** the existing canonical cycle key appears for the second
   time in the episode. The draw classifier declares cycling at three
   repetitions; collection intervenes one occurrence earlier.
4. **Action waste:** the learner proposes EndTurn while the authoritative trace
   projection reports at least one productive legal action.

The complete set of reasons is recorded. Identical canonical states are
retained at most once per episode even if they recur or satisfy multiple
reasons. All accepted eligible labels are trained, not only learner/teacher
disagreements. Disagreement rate remains a primary diagnostic.

Each iteration collects at least 20,000 valid labels and stops only after the
current reciprocal map pair is complete. It has a hard ceiling of 2,000 games.
Failure to reach the label count before the ceiling is a failed collection
stage; thresholds are not relaxed after observing the shortfall.

## Oracle preflight and contract

The oracle preflight compares exactly two deterministic bounded-search
configurations:

| Candidate | Depth | Expansion budget | Heuristic |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| baseline | 4 | 512 | current material-plus-pursuit heuristic |
| wider | 4 | 2,048 | identical heuristic |

Each candidate plays all six declared near/far conversion profiles on the 20
preflight maps assigned to that profile and from both seats: 240 games per
candidate. A candidate is eligible only when it:

- wins at least 85% pooled conversion games;
- produces identical decisions when the same state and configuration are
  queried twice;
- returns an authoritative legal command that round-trips to the recorded
  masked action for every label;
- sustains at least 10 valid labels per second in the in-process labeling
  benchmark.

Among eligible configurations, selection uses higher pooled conversion win
rate, then fewer cycling draws, then greater labeling throughput, then the
smaller expansion budget. One global oracle is frozen before DAgger collection.
No per-state teacher selection or budget tuning is permitted. If neither
candidate passes, the panel stops and search improvement becomes a separate
experiment.

Each label records oracle type, depth, expansion budget, heuristic identity,
actual expansion count, command, encoded action, and code identity. Search may
use the full engine state only because this experiment requires
`fog_of_war=false`; enabling fog fails validation until a seat-visible oracle
contract exists.

## Dataset schema and aggregation

The original imitation dataset remains immutable and is referenced by its
manifest and file hashes. Each DAgger iteration publishes a separate immutable
overlay. A row contains:

- exact policy observation and legal mask;
- learner action and authoritative learner command;
- teacher action and authoritative teacher command;
- learner/teacher disagreement;
- all eligibility reasons and the canonical state hash;
- map seed, episode seed, seat, profile, round, and decision index;
- learner checkpoint and source-run identities;
- teacher specification and expansion evidence;
- scenario, contract, encoding, dataset, code, and panel identities.

Teacher actions must be legal under the recorded mask and round-trip through
the authoritative codec. Any mismatch fails the game and prevents stage
publication. Invalid actions never fall back to EndTurn.

Iteration `k` trains on the original dataset plus overlays `1..k`. The fixed
minibatch exposure is:

- 49% original Greedy standard demonstrations;
- 21% original bounded-search conversion demonstrations;
- 30% aggregate selective-DAgger labels.

The first two sources preserve their original 70:30 ratio within the 70% base
demonstration share. DAgger labels are sampled uniformly after the per-episode
state cap; the first causal experiment does not quality-weight disagreements or
teacher scores.

## Actor training

Iteration 1 warm-starts the actor from the frozen 38,912-step PPO checkpoint.
Each later iteration warm-starts from the preceding DAgger actor. Only actor
and shared feature-extractor parameters receive masked cross-entropy gradients;
the value head is not evidence for this supervised objective and is not used by
the deployed deterministic controller.

For teacher action `a*`, legal mask `m`, and logits `z`, the loss is:

```text
L(theta) = -log softmax(mask(z_theta(observation), m))[a*]
```

The locked optimization configuration is batch size 256, learning rate
`3e-4`, at most 50 epochs, patience 5 on held-out targeted-label negative log
likelihood, model/sampler seed 227, and CUDA training with canonical CPU
publication. Every iteration records epoch metrics, phase timings, throughput,
hardware/software provenance, and exact save/reload logits.

Before training an iteration, its incoming learner generates exactly 2,000
valid held-out labels from that iteration's assigned `19,xxx,xxx` range under
the same selection and oracle contracts, stopping only after a reciprocal pair
and failing at a 200-game ceiling. Iteration `k` validates on the cumulative
held-out overlays `1..k`, just as it trains on cumulative training overlays.
Validation rows never enter an optimizer minibatch. Teacher-action accuracy is
diagnostic; closed-loop wins are authoritative.

## Evaluation and decision rules

Before DAgger collection, the starting policy is evaluated on the fixed 100-map
development bank `20,000,000-20,000,099`, both seats, Random opponent, and
forced `standard-3v3`. Iterations 1, 2, and 3 use the identical 200-game
schedule. Every draw and loss is a non-win.

Report for every candidate:

- W/L/D with Wilson 95% intervals;
- paired map-seat changes and exact sign tests;
- cycling and action-waste incidence;
- wasted EndTurn counts and ratios;
- final and peak normalized advantage;
- rounds and decisions to victory;
- seat-specific results and asymmetry;
- held-out teacher-action accuracy and disagreement by eligibility reason.

Select the candidate with the highest standard-game win rate. Ties break by
lower cycling incidence, then lower action-waste incidence, then the earlier
iteration.

The one-seed treatment succeeds only if the selected iteration produces both:

- at least a 20-percentage-point win-rate gain over the newly measured starting
  baseline, or at least 65% standard wins; and
- at least a 50% relative reduction in cycling incidence.

A 20-point gain authorizes replication; it is not the winning-model milestone.
The milestone remains at least 65% wins for every independent replicate and at
least 70% pooled. A successful treatment is repeated with three independently
initialized learners and learner-specific collection before any final-bank use.
That replication is a subsequent experiment: it must either produce matched
starting checkpoints under the locked BC-to-PPO protocol or explicitly define
and audit a different common starting condition before collecting labels.

Interpretation is fixed:

- Better held-out imitation and better games: replicate selective DAgger.
- Better imitation without better games: advance to candidate/afterstate
  representation work.
- Poor imitation: investigate model capacity, label ambiguity, and oracle
  quality before changing the action contract.
- Regression: retain the negative result and do not tune against the same
  development schedule.

## Interface architecture

### Engine boundary

`TacticalV2DuelEnv` gains an optional pre-action learner-decision observer. The
environment constructs an immutable decision context after decoding the
external action and before applying it. The context exposes the pre-action
state, seat-relative observation, legal mask, learner action and command,
registries required by the authoritative codec, and episode metadata.

The observer depends on an `IActionOracle` interface and an evidence sink. It
may classify eligibility, query the oracle, and emit a label. It cannot replace
the learner command. With no observer installed, transition order, rewards,
replays, and outcomes are byte-for-byte unchanged.

### Python boundary

A focused DAgger module owns panel DTOs, overlays, validation, aggregation, and
decision rules. The existing imitation module continues to own materialized
batches, masked loss, actor optimization, and controller publication. The
sampler is extended through an explicit source-mixture interface rather than a
DAgger-specific conditional embedded in the trainer.

The panel runner owns orchestration, not game semantics. All seed ranges,
selection thresholds, mixture weights, model sources, and teacher parameters
come from immutable, hash-bound definitions.

This observer is research instrumentation. It is not an inference-time engine
API and is absent during ordinary play and evaluation.

## Restart safety, errors, and logging

Every stage uses a deterministic staging path and publishes by atomic rename
only after physically reopening and validating all outputs. Completed reuse
requires exact repository, definition, scenario, contract, encoding, learner,
teacher, dataset, and schedule identities.

Collection fails closed on:

- illegal or non-round-tripping learner or teacher commands;
- observation, mask, or state-hash inconsistency;
- seed overlap or incomplete reciprocal pairs;
- repeated-state cap violations or duplicate row identities;
- source checkpoint or dataset changes;
- teacher nondeterminism;
- missing traces, replays, manifests, or content hashes;
- repository changes during a stage.

Interrupted diagnostics remain inspectable but cannot appear complete.

Structured progress is flushed during oracle preflight, collection, training,
and evaluation. Collection events report completed games, accepted labels,
eligibility counts, disagreement counts, teacher expansions, labels per second,
elapsed time, and ETA. Training retains the established per-epoch phase timing.
Operators never infer completion by opening partially written artifacts.

## Verification

Before production compute, tests must prove:

### Engine

- the observer receives the exact pre-action state, observation, mask, learner
  action, and command;
- enabling a passive observer does not change commands, transitions, rewards,
  terminal outcomes, traces, or replays;
- the learner action, not the teacher label, advances the game;
- every teacher command is legal and round-trips through tactical-v2 encoding;
- conversion, positive-advantage, second-repetition, and wasted-EndTurn
  eligibility match authoritative diagnostics;
- fixed oracle configuration is deterministic and respects its expansion
  budget;
- fog-of-war scenarios fail the oracle contract.

### Python

- immutable overlay manifests and content hashes;
- disjoint seed namespaces and reciprocal schedules;
- one retained canonical state per episode;
- exact 49/21/30 source exposure, including undersized-stratum cycling;
- no validation row enters training;
- actor warm-start, CUDA-to-CPU publication, exact save/reload logits, and fresh
  provenance;
- restart reuse reopens physical artifacts and launches zero new games;
- evaluation summaries are reconstructed from retained traces and replays.

### End-to-end smoke

Use seeds `18,990,000` (`standard-3v3`) and `18,990,001`
(`conversion-3v1-near`) from both seats for exactly four collection games.
Train one CPU epoch on the accepted smoke labels, publish and reload an actor,
then evaluate standard games from both seats on seeds `18,990,002` and
`18,990,003`, exactly four evaluation games. Rerun the command to prove complete
physical reuse and zero new games. Then run the full Python suite, genuine
.NET 8 engine suite, Unity compile check, and any affected Unity tests before
production collection.

## Design decision

Implement a three-iteration selective-DAgger experiment beginning from the
physically audited 38,912-step seed-227 actor. Preserve ordinary behavior with
the immutable original demonstrations, train on high-confidence teacher labels
from learner-induced failure states, and judge success exclusively through
paired annihilation outcomes and cycling reduction.
